The Trilemma of Informal Savings

Why $50 Billion of community capital is broken, risky, and invisible

02 • Problem
Problem 01 • Risk

Default & "Runaway" Syndrome

In traditional Ajo, members who take turns #1 or #2 frequently default, delay payments, or vanish entirely once their lump sum is collected.

Data Point: 18% to 24% of informal circles suffer late payments or painful social default annually.
Problem 02 • Friction

Organiser Burnout ("Wahala")

Circle administrators spend 15+ hours each month tracking spreadsheets, confirming fake bank alerts, making awkward reminder calls, and arbitrating bitter disputes.

The Result: Captains abandon managing groups after 2–3 cycles due to immense social and operational exhaustion.
Problem 03 • Exclusion

The Credit Reputation Blindspot

A market woman or salaried engineer can punctually save ₦500,000 monthly for 5 years in an Ajo club, but this spotless track record is never reported to formal credit bureaus.

Economic Penalty: They remain officially "unscoreable" and locked out of commercial bank mortgages, auto loans, and working capital.

The Solution: Bank-Secured Social Escrow

Replacing social friction with automated open-banking rails and enforceable trust

03 • Solution
Pillar 1

BVN & NIN Identity Lock

Every circle participant is biometrically verified via Open Banking (Mono). Default history creates a permanent record, eliminating ghost members and impersonation.

Pillar 2

Automated Direct Debit Sweeps

No manual bank transfers. Direct debit mandates automatically pull exact contributions from members' salary accounts at 6:00 AM on scheduled contribution dates.

Pillar 3

Non-Custodial Escrow Disbursal

Powered by CBN-licensed PSSPs (Paylode). Àjọṣe never touches or pools user deposits; funds route directly bank-to-bank into the designated payout recipient's account.

Pillar 4 • Moat

The Ajo Credit Score

On-time payments build a dynamic, portable credit passport. High-score members unlock earlier payout turns, lower fees, and access to elite, high-value nationwide circles.

✓

Zero Administrative Wahala for Circle Organisers

Organizers set the amount, schedule, and turns once. Àjọṣe manages sweeps, ledger reconciliation, and payouts hands-free.

100% AUTOMATED
