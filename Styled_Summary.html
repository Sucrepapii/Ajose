Àjọṣe

Executive Summary

Company Name: Àjọṣe
Website: www.ajose.ng
Sector: FinTech / WealthTech (Digital Peer-to-Peer Savings)
Core Offering: Secure, digitized rotational savings (Ajo/Esusu) powered by open-banking and automated identity verification.

1. The Overview

Àjọṣe is a modern financial technology platform that digitizes and secures the centuries-old African tradition of rotational savings (Ajo/Esusu). By leveraging open-banking API infrastructure and rigorous identity compliance, Àjọṣe transforms informal, trust-based peer-to-peer savings groups into highly secure, automated, and credit-building financial ecosystems. We empower individuals, entrepreneurs, and communities to pool funds and access lump-sum capital without the barriers of traditional credit systems.

2. The Problem

Across Nigeria and the broader African continent, millions of people rely on informal rotational savings clubs to raise bulk capital for businesses, rent, or personal projects. However, the traditional Ajo model is plagued by three critical issues:

  • High Default Risk & Fraud: Reliance on verbal trust often leads to members absconding after receiving their payout turn.
  • Administrative Friction: Managing collections, tracking turns, and disbursing funds manually is tedious and prone to human error.
  • Financial Exclusion: Participants build strong repayment histories within their informal circles, but this data is never captured by formal credit bureaus, locking them out of the formal credit economy.

3. The Solution

Àjọṣe eliminates the friction and risk of informal savings by providing a fully digitized, secure, and automated platform. We act as the digital escrow and administrative layer for rotational savings circles. Members can easily form or join circles, agree on contribution amounts, and automate their payments, ensuring that every participant receives their payout on time, without the administrative "wahala" of manual follow-ups.

4. Key Technological Innovations

Àjọṣe utilizes cutting-edge integrations to ensure a zero-trust environment becomes a high-trust ecosystem:

  • Automated Identity Verification: We utilize Mono's Open-Banking engine to instantly verify Bank Verification Numbers (BVN) and National Identity Numbers (NIN), ensuring every participant is a verified, trackable entity.
  • Direct Debit Mandates: Contributions are not left to chance. Through secure banking APIs, scheduled contributions are automatically swept from members' accounts, drastically reducing late payments.
  • Proprietary Credit Scoring: Members earn a dynamic credit score based on their contribution punctuality. Defaulters receive a negative score and risk being blacklisted, while highly-rated members unlock access to premium, high-value circles and earlier payout turns.
  • Non-Custodial Escrow Disbursal: Funds are routed securely via regulated payment partners (PSSP), ensuring the platform remains compliant while guaranteeing automated, instant payouts when a cycle completes.

5. Target Market

Our primary market includes the unbanked, underbanked, and upwardly mobile digitally-native Nigerians. This encompasses:

  • Civil Servants & Government Employees: Relying on fixed, consistent monthly salaries who use rotational savings as a disciplined mechanism to generate bulk capital for building projects, land purchases, or children's tuition.
  • SME Owners & Market Traders: Seeking interest-free, lump-sum capital to purchase inventory or expand operations without the crippling interest rates of traditional loans.
  • Working Professionals & Freelancers: Looking for structured saving mechanisms for large personal expenses (rent, vehicles, travel, or weddings).
  • Existing Ajo Communities & Cooperatives: Social groups, market associations, and office departments looking to modernize their current manual operations and eliminate administrative headaches.

6. The Value Proposition

  • For the Saver: Guaranteed payouts, enforced discipline, and the ability to build a verifiable financial reputation.
  • For the Economy: Migrating billions of Naira from the informal shadow economy into structured, trackable, and credit-worthy financial systems.

7. Strategic Vision

Àjọṣe’s vision extends beyond digitization; we aim to become the definitive bridge between informal community savings and formal credit infrastructure. As we scale, the transactional data and repayment histories generated on Àjọṣe will serve as an alternative credit-scoring oracle, enabling financial institutions to confidently offer micro-loans and credit products to previously invisible demographics.
