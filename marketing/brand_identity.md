# Ajose — Brand Identity & Messaging Document

**Product:** Ajose  
**Category:** Digital Rotational Contribution Scheme Management (Ajo / Esusu)  
**Target Audience:** Ajo admins + active contributors  
**Market:** Nigeria (primary)  
**Session Date:** September 10, 2026  

---

## 1. Product Positioning

Ajose is the modern digital platform for managing voluntary rotational contribution schemes — what Nigerians know as Ajo or Esusu. It replaces the informal WhatsApp group, the shared spreadsheet, and the handwritten ledger with a transparent, automated, and trustworthy system.

**The two users Ajose serves:**
1. **The Admin** — the person running the group, collecting contributions, managing the order of payout, and chasing defaulters.
2. **The Contributor** — the person paying in every cycle and waiting for their turn to collect.

---

## 2. Core Pain Points Ajose Solves

| Pain Point | Who Feels It |
| :--- | :--- |
| Missing contributions — someone doesn't pay and the collector has to top up | Admin |
| No clear record of who has paid and who hasn't | Admin |
| Arguments about whose turn it is to collect | Both |
| Money going missing or being mismanaged | Contributor |
| No reminder system — members forget to pay | Admin |
| No transparency — contributor doesn't know the group's financial state | Contributor |
| Spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups are messy and unreliable | Both |

## 3. Tagline Options

## 4. Recommended Brand Tagline

### **"Turn by turn, no wahala."**

**Why this wins:**
- Instantly recognisable to every Nigerian who has ever been in an Ajo group.
- "Turn by turn" carries the entire value proposition — fairness, order, everyone gets their payout.
- "No wahala" handles every pain point in two words — no stress, no arguments, no missing money, no confusion.
- Short enough to fit on a t-shirt, a banner, or an app icon splash screen.
- Works spoken out loud as well as written — important for a product marketed in Nigeria where word of mouth is king.

## 6. Brand Voice Direction

| Attribute | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tone** | Warm, confident, Nigerian-authentic — not stiff or corporate |
| **Language** | Plain English with natural Nigerian expressions (*Ajo*, *Esusu*, *wahala*, *turn by turn*) where they add colour |
| **Feeling** | Like a trusted friend who runs a tight ship — dependable but approachable |
| **Avoid** | Overly technical language, Western fintech jargon, anything that sounds foreign or distant |
