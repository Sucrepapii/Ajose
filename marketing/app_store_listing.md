## Full Description

**Running an Ajo group shouldn’t give you headaches.** 

Ajose is the modern digital platform for managing voluntary rotational contribution schemes — what Nigerians know and trust as **Ajo**, **Esusu**, or **Osusu**. It replaces the informal WhatsApp group, the shared spreadsheet, and handwritten ledgers with a transparent, automated, and trustworthy system.

Whether you are an **Ajo Admin** organizing a group of colleagues and business partners, or an active **Contributor** saving towards your next milestone, Ajose makes sure every naira and every round is accounted for.

---

### Why Ajo Groups Love Ajose:

#### For Group Admins:
- **No More Chasing Payments on WhatsApp:** Automatic collection reminders sent to members via SMS and email.
- **Fair & Transparent Payout Schedules:** Automated turn-assignment and round sequence tracking.
- **Clear Financial Records:** Real-time ledger showing exactly who has paid, who is pending, and payout history.
- **Zero Calculation Stress:** Automated round totals and contribution calculations.

#### For Contributors:
- **Your Money Is Safe:** Full visibility into group performance and verification status.
- **Your Turn Is Tracked:** Instant notifications when your payout round arrives (*"Your turn don reach!"*).
- **Instant Payment Receipts:** Real-time confirmation whenever your contribution is logged (*"E don pay ✓"*).
- **Peace of Mind:** Clear, audit-proof history accessible from your smartphone 24/7.
