# Brand Identity & Marketing Visual Rules

- **Always Pair Logo with Brand Name (Combination Mark):** All marketing visuals, posters, social media banners, promotional assets, and pitch deck mockups for Àjọṣe must combine the circular emblem (interlocking arms around the traditional savings pot) with the brand name typography: **ÀJỌṢE** (with proper diacritics) and optional descriptor (`DIGITAL AJO & THRIFT` or `AUTOMATED SAVINGS CIRCLES`).
- **No Standalone Pictorial Marks in Marketing:** Never generate or deploy isolated pictorial marks without the brand name on marketing collateral at this brand life cycle stage. Visual identity must always directly build name recognition.
